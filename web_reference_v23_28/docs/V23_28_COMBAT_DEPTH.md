# V23.28 Combat Depth & Loot Flow

## New combat depth
- Stagger meter on enemies.
- Crits, combo finishers and skills build stagger faster.
- When stagger breaks, enemies are briefly stunned.
- Bosses require far more stagger and are immune during final enraged phase.
- Fast / heavy / ranged enemy archetypes now behave differently.
- Wisp, Crystal Imp and Fireling-style enemies can fight at range and kite.
- Enemy projectiles respect dash i-frames and world collision.

## Targeting
- Basic attack still auto-acquires a nearby target.
- After a kill, the client quickly attempts to acquire the next nearby target.
- A short ASSIST indicator makes automatic targeting explicit.

## Loot flow
- Nearby drops softly magnetize toward the player.
- Auto-loot pickup radius increased slightly.
- Loot magnet is restrained during very busy combat.
- Rare drop beams and pity remain.

## Readability
- Stagger bar appears under relevant enemy HP.
- Stunned enemies receive a clear world label and target state.
- FLOW visually enhances the action cluster.
