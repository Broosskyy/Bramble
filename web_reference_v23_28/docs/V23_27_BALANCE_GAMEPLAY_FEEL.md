# V23.27 Balance & Gameplay Feel

## Combat rhythm
- Basic attacks now form short class-aware chains.
- Melee third hit acts as a finisher.
- Bow and mage receive periodic precision/empowered shots.
- Attacking without a selected target auto-acquires the nearest valid enemy.
- FLOW meter builds through hits and kills.
- High momentum gives small movement/power benefits.
- At 100 FLOW a short 6-second combat rush activates.

## Survivability
- Potions heal a percentage of max HP instead of a flat value.
- Potions have an 8 second cooldown.
- Incoming damage now uses enemy-vs-player level difference plus defense mitigation.
- Guard states meaningfully reduce incoming damage.
- Dangerous over-level targets are marked in the target panel.

## Enemies
- Aggro range is lower for ordinary mobs and higher for elites/bosses.
- Small same-species packs can assist each other.
- Fast and heavy enemy archetypes use different windup/cooldown rhythms.
- Enemy movement still respects V23.26 world collision.

## Progression / farming
- Rewards scale down when greatly over-leveling old zones and scale modestly upward against stronger enemies.
- Kill chains award periodic gold/EXP bonuses.
- High-loot pity: after a dry streak, a rare equipment drop is forced.
- Ordinary respawn pacing tightened to 8–14 seconds.
- Dynamic elite encounters occur somewhat more often.

Goal: retain the approachable anime-MMO feel while making combat active enough for mobile action-RPG play.
