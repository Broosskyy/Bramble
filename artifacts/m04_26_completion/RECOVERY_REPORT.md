2. The Wayfarer helmet and armor have eight idle directions, but canonical movement and attack pose atlases do not exist. Runtime bob/lunge and weapon swing prove attachment timing, not final pose art.
3. Moorling kit60 has four authored directions and action frames, but no legitimate eight-direction movement/combat set. Its asymmetric art must not be mirrored to claim coverage.
4. Mobile touch behavior, overdraw, thermals, and frame pacing cannot be approved without a physical Android device.
5. An Android SDK directory and Java tools exist, but `adb` is unavailable, no Godot Android export templates were found, and the project has no `export_presets.cfg`; therefore no test APK was produced.
