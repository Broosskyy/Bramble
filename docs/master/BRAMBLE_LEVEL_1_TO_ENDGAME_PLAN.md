# BRAMBLE Level 1–60 and Endgame Plan

Status: **PRODUCTION AUTHORITY — PROGRESSION PLAN**  
Applies to: launch level cap 60  
Dependency: `BRAMBLE_MASTER_CONCEPT_VNEXT.md`

## 1. Progression contract

The level journey teaches one concept at a time, then combines it under pressure. A level must produce at least one of: a new decision, a visible/mechanical power change, access to a new place/activity, or mastery evidence. XP alone is not a milestone.

Target first-character time to cap is **55–75 active hours**, adjusted from telemetry without changing unlock order. Main quests provide the reliable route; side and repeatable content enrich, never block, the critical path. No purchase accelerates combat power.

## 7. Endgame at level 60

### Daily loops

Target: one useful reward in 10–20 minutes; a player may stop after any packet.

- three-choice regional contract board, with one free reroll and no paid rerolls;
- one rotating dungeon objective with first-completion bonus;
- open-world miniboss/public-event target with contribution protection;
- crafting/gathering order and companion bond activity;
- optional PvP first-match objective with mode-appropriate reward;
- social/help objective completed through mentor queue or guild activity.

Daily rewards use soft caps and broad source choice. Missing a day does not destroy a streak or permanently lose power.

### Weekly loops

- six-dungeon rotation with a selectable reward cache;
- three-raid progression/reclear path with encounter lockouts, checkpoint/rejoin, and bad-luck protection;
- regional reputation and story epilogue steps;
- ranked PvP placement/activity goal with normalized competition;
- guild cooperative target without mandatory attendance windows;
- T6 deterministic upgrade currency cap, with multiple PvE/PvP acquisition routes;
- rotating collection, pet/partner/fairy bond, and cosmetic prestige objectives.

Weekly reset state, caps, lockouts, and remaining opportunities are visible before entry. Duplicate rewards convert through a disclosed, useful path.

### Long-term loops

- master both class Mantles and multiple viable loadouts;
- complete T6 equipment sets and deterministic upgrades;
- collect 8 pets, 4 partners, 6 fairies, cosmetics, titles, achievements, and regional completion;
- progress raid mastery, challenge modifiers, PvP rank, guild goals, and mentor recognition;
- pursue prestige presentation that never obscures class, danger, or competitive readability.

Endgame avoids infinite mandatory stat escalation. Seasonal activity adds alternate challenges and cosmetics while preserving earned launch power.

## 8. Cap increases and catch-up

A cap increase ships only when the new band has a complete story route, accessible gear bridge, class/Mantle balance pass, group content, mobile/device proof, server/persistence migration, and endgame replacement plan.

Rules:

1. Announce cap, conversion, lockout, and item relevance before release.
2. Existing T6 remains a valid entry baseline; it is not deleted or rendered immediately useless.
3. Provide an account-wide campaign skip only after one character completes the story; skipped teaching remains replayable.
4. Raise rested XP and main-quest XP for levels below the newest band; never sell the catch-up multiplier.
5. Supply deterministic bridge gear through story, crafting, and mentor activities.
6. Preserve cosmetics, collections, Mantles, companions, achievements, and social progress.
7. Compress obsolete currencies/upgrade steps with transparent conversion and no value loss.
8. Keep prior dungeons/raids relevant through scaled rotations, materials, cosmetics, and mentor queues, not compulsory full reclears.
9. New or returning players reach current-group eligibility through a bounded path measured in days, not months.
10. Never use a cap increase to reset PvP fairness, invalidate purchases, or hide content removal.

## 9. Progression acceptance

Telemetry must measure time-to-first-combat, death/retry, quest abandonment, level duration, build diversity, matchmaking wait, disconnect recovery, reward comprehension, economy inflation, and touch-error rate. It may tune numbers and routing but may not silently alter odds, target minors, personalize prices, or create frustration for monetization.

The plan is launch-complete only when all counts reconcile, every unlock has a recoverable quest/state path, all four classes and eight Mantles pass solo/group/mobile tests, and level 60 transitions into understandable daily, weekly, and long-term goals.
